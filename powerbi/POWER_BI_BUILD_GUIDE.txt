--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1: LOAD & CONFIGURE ENVIRONMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In Power BI Desktop, click 'Get Data' -> 'Text/CSV'.
2. Select 'data/processed/sales_transactions_clean.csv' and load. Rename table to 'Sales'.
3. Import Custom Theme:
   - Go to 'View' tab -> Themes dropdown -> 'Browse for themes...'.
   - Select 'powerbi/sales_insights_theme.json'.
   - This instantly applies dark slate canvas (#0B0F19), glassmorphic cards (#131C2E),
     rounded borders (10px), drop shadows, and executive color palettes.
4. Create Date Dimension:
   - Modeling tab -> New Table -> Paste 'Date' table DAX from DAX_measures.txt.
   - Mark as Date Table (select Date[Date]).
   - Sort 'Month Short' by 'Month Number'.
   - Relate Date[Date] (1) -> Sales[order_date] (*).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2: PAGE LAYOUTS & WIREFRAMES (16:9 Canvas)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PAGE 1 — EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
+ Top KPI Ribbon (New Card Visual):
  - Card 1: Total Sales (₹750.7M) | Subtitle: [Sales KPI Subtitle] ("▲ +14.2% vs LY")
  - Card 2: Total Profit (₹169.4M) | Subtitle: [Profit KPI Subtitle] ("▲ +11.8% vs LY")
  - Card 3: Profit Margin % (22.6%) | Subtitle: [Margin Health Badge] ("🟢 Healthy")
  - Card 4: Average Order Value (₹50,045) | Subtitle: [Orders] ("15,000 Orders")
+ Top Slicers: Date[Year] and Sales[channel]
+ Monthly Sales & Profit Trend (Line and Stacked Column / Area):
  - X-Axis: Date[Year Month]
  - Line Y-Axis: [Total Sales] (Cyan #38BDF8 with glowing data markers)
  - Column Y-Axis: [Total Profit] (Emerald #10B981)
+ Category Breakdown (Clustered Bar Chart):
  - Y-Axis: Sales[category]
  - X-Axis: [Total Sales] (Data labels enabled, formatted in ₹M)
+ Channel Breakdown (Donut Chart):
  - Legend: Sales[channel] | Values: [Total Sales] (Online ~60% vs Store ~40%)

PAGE 2 — PRODUCT & MARGIN DEEP DIVE
+ Sales vs. Profit Margin Matrix (Scatter Plot):
  - X-Axis: [Total Sales]
  - Y-Axis: [Profit Margin %]
  - Size: [Units Sold] | Details: Sales[product]
  - Actionable: Instantly flags high-revenue products with sub-18% margins!
+ Top 10 Products by Sales (Horizontal Bar Chart):
  - Conditional Bar Color: Field Value -> [Color_Margin_Status]
+ Low-Margin Alert Table:
  - Columns: product, category, Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Average Discount %
  - Filter: [Profit Margin %] < 0.18 with conditional formatting on discount %

PAGE 3 — CUSTOMER & CHANNEL DYNAMICS
+ Channel Comparison (Clustered Column Chart):
  - X-Axis: Sales[channel] | Y-Axis: [Total Sales], [Total Profit]
+ Payment Mode Analysis (Treemap / Donut):
  - Category: Sales[payment_mode] | Values: [Total Sales]
+ Customer Concentration Pareto (Table):
  - Columns: customer_id, Total Orders, Total Sales, Total Profit, AOV

PAGE 4 — REGIONAL GEOGRAPHY & DRILL-THROUGH
+ State-Level Performance (Filled Map / Shape Map or Matrix):
  - Location: Sales[state] | Color saturation: [Total Sales]
+ Regional KPI Cards: North, South, West, East breakdown
+ Drill-Through Page ('City Drillthrough'):
  - Enable drill-through on Sales[state] to explore city-level metrics.
